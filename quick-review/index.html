01

Order & Degree

Order

The highest derivative that appears.

Degree

The power of that highest derivative, once the DE is a polynomial in its derivatives.

Before reading the degree, clean the equation up:

  • Radicals or fractional powers: raise both sides to a power (the LCD of the denominators). Example: (y′′′)2/3=1+y′′→(y′′′)4=(1+y′′)3(y''')^{2/3}=\sqrt{1+y''}\to(y''')^4=(1+y'')^3(y′′′)2/3=1+y′′​→(y′′′)4=(1+y′′)3.
  • Negative powers: multiply through. Example: (y′′)−1+⋯→(y'')^{-1}+\dots\to(y′′)−1+⋯→ multiply by y′′y''y′′.
  • Derivative inside ln, sin, e: isolate it and invert. Example: ln⁡y′′=u⇒y′′=eu\ln y''=u\Rightarrow y''=e^{u}lny′′=u⇒y′′=eu, and sin⁡y′=x⇒y′=sin⁡−1x\sin y'=x\Rightarrow y'=\sin^{-1}xsiny′=x⇒y′=sin−1x.
  • Careful: a big power on a lower derivative does not count. Only the highest one matters.
02

Linear or Nonlinear?

A DE is linear in yyy when it can be written as

an(x) y(n)+⋯+a1(x) y′+a0(x) y=f(x)a_n(x)\,y^{(n)}+\dots+a_1(x)\,y'+a_0(x)\,y=f(x)an​(x)y(n)+⋯+a1​(x)y′+a0​(x)y=f(x)

Look only at the parts with yyy. It is nonlinear if you see:

  • a power other than 1: y2y^2y2, (y′)3(y')^3(y′)3, y′\sqrt{y'}y′​
  • a product: y y′y\,y'yy′, y y′′y\,y''yy′′
  • yyy inside a function: sin⁡y\sin ysiny, eye^{y}ey, cos⁡(x+y)\cos(x+y)cos(x+y)
  • yyy in a denominator: 1x+y\dfrac{1}{x+y}x+y1​

Coefficients can be any function of xxx, such as exe^xex, x2x^2x2 or sin⁡x\sin xsinx. That's still linear.

03

Verifying a Solution

  1. Differentiate the proposed solution as many times as the DE needs.
  2. Substitute everything into the DE.
  3. Simplify. If LHS = RHS for all xxx, it's a solution. If not, say so and show why.

Implicit solutions (F(x,y)=CF(x,y)=CF(x,y)=C): take the differential of both sides (dC=0dC=0dC=0) and show you get the DE back.

05

DE of a Family of Curves

  1. Write the equation of the family.
  2. Sort the constants: arbitrary (changes from curve to curve) vs. fixed (given: r, a, Q, k, α).
  3. Differentiate once per arbitrary constant.
  4. Eliminate the arbitrary constants. The fixed ones stay in the answer.

Want to see it? Every family is live in the Graph Lab.

Which Solving Method?

Separable

ME 31, 32, 37, 38

Recognize

Can be arranged as f(x) dx+g(y) dy=0f(x)\,dx+g(y)\,dy=0f(x)dx+g(y)dy=0

Do

Integrate each side: ∫f dx+∫g dy=C\int f\,dx+\int g\,dy=C∫fdx+∫gdy=C

Homogeneous

ME 33, 39

Recognize

MMM, NNN homogeneous of the same degree (depends only on y/xy/xy/x)

Do

Let y=vxy=vxy=vx, dy=v dx+x dvdy=v\,dx+x\,dvdy=vdx+xdv, then separate

Exact

Recognize

∂M∂y=∂N∂x\dfrac{\partial M}{\partial y}=\dfrac{\partial N}{\partial x}∂y∂M​=∂x∂N​

Do

F=∫M dx+g(y)F=\int M\,dx+g(y)F=∫Mdx+g(y); find ggg from Fy=NF_y=NFy​=N

ME 34, 40

Recognize

y′+P(x) y=Q(x)y'+P(x)\,y=Q(x)y′+P(x)y=Q(x)

Do

IF =e∫P dx=e^{\int P\,dx}=e∫Pdx;   y⋅IF=∫Q⋅IF dx+C\;y\cdot\text{IF}=\int Q\cdot\text{IF}\,dx+Cy⋅IF=∫Q⋅IFdx+C

Bernoulli

ME 36, 42

Recognize

y′+P(x) y=Q(x) yny'+P(x)\,y=Q(x)\,y^ny′+P(x)y=Q(x)yn
