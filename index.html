Heads-up

Five spots in the papers look mistyped.

Each one is flagged on its item and solved using the most likely intended version. Know these before you compare answers with classmates.

  • LT #15“4′” read as 4y′
  • LT #19c₃ cos ωt repeats c₁ cos ωt
  • LT #13only works with “−”, not “+”
  • ME #25the value of α is blank
  • ME #33second dx should be dy
